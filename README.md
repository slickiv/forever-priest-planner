# WoW Forever Priest Throughput

A static, client-side Priest gear and healing throughput planner. Armor slots accept cloth, accessories and held items may be any armor type, and Wowhead remains the live item and loot-table reference. The item planner completes stats by item name or Wowhead ID. Gear and talent inputs are stored in browser local storage.

## Run and publish

Open `index.html` in a modern browser or serve this directory with a static file server. GitHub Pages publishes the repository root through its Actions workflow.

## Spell data and formulae

Spell rank, level, base range, cost, and Wowhead spell ID records are in `spells.js`; the user supplied tooltip observations are in `observations/annadra-level-21-22-tooltips.csv`. The records use the Forever Priest client spellbook and Wowhead Forever entries. Numeric prices from [Warcraft Forever's Priest spell recorder](https://warcraftforever.games/classes/priest/spells) are labeled beta recorder quotes, not live quotes. Lesser Heal, Renew, and Heal Rank 4 prices are separately tagged reference amounts from [UrGameTips](https://urgametips.com/wow-forever/priest-guides/spells/) and [X'yera's beta-observed trainer list](https://wowforever.ninja/npcs/6014-xyera/). Lesser Heal Rank 1 is shown as a free starter rank based on the spell guide's level-1 row with no trainer fee. Renew Rank 10 is learned from the Codex of Renew X manual; its purchase price is not listed. Null prices elsewhere remain unknown rather than free.

Display clarity checks: keep each main healing value aligned with its game tooltip; show Renew's tooltip total first, followed by base total and healing per tick; don't repeat “Instant” alongside its 1.5s GCD; place spell-wide mechanics in one description per spell card rather than repeating them in rank rows; group training source links once per spell card; and avoid repeating totals or metrics. When an expected value equals its mean, label it “Same as mean” instead of printing the same number twice. Review the rendered table at narrow and wide widths after display changes.

For each rank, the formula is:

1. Start with that rank's client base healing minimum and maximum, plus per-level base growth where its metadata has been verified and the rank has not reached its cap.
2. Add effective bonus healing: `(gear healing + other unlisted healing + Spiritual Guidance bonus) × rank coefficient × downrank retention`.
3. Apply relevant healing talents, round the minimum down and maximum up, then use the arithmetic midpoint of the displayed range for means.
4. Divide by effective cast time or mana cost for throughput and efficiency. Instants use a 1.5-second GCD; channels use their channel duration. Group spells use the target count, and shields are reported as absorb.

The Forever downrank rule measured from levels 18–30 is full coefficient for 17 levels after learning, then 5 percentage points less per level. Values above level 30 extrapolate that observed rule. Lesser Heal rank 1 has an additional measured 15 percentage point retention reduction. Heal R2's generated client effect has a level-22 midpoint of 375, 2.9 healing growth per level, 13.47826123% variance, and a level-27 cap. Its base range is rounded from `midpoint × (1 ± variance/2)`, yielding 350–400 at level 22 and 363–416 at level 27. With the prior +101 bonus-healing input, the formula yields 436–487; the supplied in-game tooltip is 439–490, so an empirical +3 rank-specific adjustment is retained. This does not change the gear/stat total or any other spell.

Healing coefficients are read from Forever client/Wowhead data where provided: common 1.5-second casts use 0.429; 3-second casts use 0.857; Lesser Heal has rank coefficients 0.429/0.571/0.714; Renew uses 1.0 total across five ticks; Prayer of Healing 0.286 per target; Holy Nova 0.107; Prayer of Mending 0.429 per successful heal; Circle of Healing 0.214 per target; Penance 0.19 per bolt across three bolts; and Power Word: Shield 0.1. The racial direct heals use 0.429. These coefficients multiply bonus healing only; base heal amounts are added separately. Penance links use the cast ID and its healing/growth uses separate bolt-effect IDs. Lightwell's heal-over-time coefficient is not exposed in the client, so it is currently calculated with no bonus-healing scaling. Circle of Healing uses its level-60 formula range and 56% base-mana cost, converted with a 1,376 level-60 Priest base-mana assumption; its acquisition is unverified.

Critical heal expectation uses a 150% critical heal: `mean × (1 + crit chance × 0.5)`. Crit chance is the entered character-sheet spell crit plus Holy Specialization for Holy-school spells, applicable Renewed Hope, and ready Inner Focus for non-periodic eligible spells. Renew can crit in Forever. Renew's displayed range, healing value, expected value, and critical-heal value are per tick (five ticks over 15 seconds); its complete 5-tick total remains available as a secondary value and is used for total-per-mana efficiency. Lightwell's critical-heal column is also a single tick. Expected healing for periodic effects remains the full spell/click total adjusted for expected crit contribution. Divine Aegis and Inspiration are limited to non-periodic critical heals. Divine Aegis does not stack and is capped at 1,200 per target; for Penance, any one or more critical bolts produce one per-bolt shield on the same target. Penance's +25 percentage-point Inner Focus interaction across all three bolts remains unverified. The table displays range, cast, mana, mean healing/absorb per cast time and mana, critical chance and single-event critical amount, expected throughput and mana efficiency, Holy Binding's extra self-heal, expected Divine Aegis absorb, and Inspiration's armor bonus/proc chance. Every column header sorts ranks within its spell; card open state and sort state persist across input changes and reloads.

Talents that affect modeled outputs include Twin Disciplines, Improved Power Word: Shield, Improved Renew, Holy Specialization, Divine Fury, Spiritual Healing, Spiritual Guidance, Renewed Hope, Divine Aegis, Inspiration, Improved Healing, Mental Agility, Soul Warding, Litany of Light, Inner Focus, Searing Light's ready Holy Nova refund, Power Infusion, Spirit of Redemption's free casts, and Holy Binding. Spiritual Guidance's Spirit contribution is floored to a whole point. Holy Binding estimates the additional self-heal as half of Flash Heal when the cast targets someone else; that added component is treated as scaling with the Flash Heal's expected crit result. Renewed Hope uses the selected Weakened Soul state. Inspiration displays its armor gain and per-target proc chance but does not estimate uptime without a cast rotation. Proc talents are modeled as ready/not-ready states, not proc probabilities. Talents that only change damage, range, pushback, hit chance, or mana regeneration do not change the cast's healing amount. Blessed Recovery depends on incoming qualifying damage; damage-linked healing from Vampiric Embrace also depends on damage dealt, so neither is included in this fixed-spell throughput table. No overhealing, proc timing, healing taken, interruptions, or fight duration is modeled.

Forever-specific Priest updates can change the spellbook: [Priest changes](https://theforeverera.com/en/classes/priest/), [Forever Priest talent records](https://talentsforever.com/priest/talents), [downranking measurements and formula](https://foreverchanges.pro/downranking), and [Wowhead Forever Heal rank 2](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2055/heal), and [the generated client effect record](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/ae9b8c23a94aafd6f42876030d8ba07ff230e960/sim/core/spelldata/spells_auto_gen.go#L1614). Healing ranges and inferred assumptions should be rechecked against in-game tooltips after game updates.
