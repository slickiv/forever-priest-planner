# WoW Forever Priest Throughput

A static, client-side Priest gear, healing, and damage throughput planner. Armor slots accept cloth, accessories and held items may be any armor type, and Wowhead remains the live item and loot-table reference. The item planner completes stats by item name or Wowhead ID. Gear and talent inputs are stored in browser local storage.

## Run and publish

Open `index.html` in a modern browser or serve this directory with a static file server. GitHub Pages publishes the repository root through its Actions workflow.

## Spell data and formulae

Spell rank, level, base range, cost, and Wowhead spell ID records are in `spells.js`; user-supplied tooltip observations are in `observations/annadra-level-21-22-tooltips.csv`. Spell effects use the Forever client spellbook and Wowhead Forever entries. Training prices in the UI are published list-price references from [UrGameTips](https://urgametips.com/wow-forever/priest-guides/spells/), before discounts; they are not claimed as in-game observations. Lesser Heal R2–R3 trainer prices remain flagged because published acquisition sources differ. Lesser Heal R1 is a free starter rank. Lightwell is included as a non-talent spell; its trainer acquisition and price are not modeled. Renew R10 is learned from the Codex of Renew X manual; its purchase price is unlisted. Unknown prices remain unknown, not free.

Display clarity checks: keep each main healing value aligned with its game tooltip; show Renew and Penance total-per-cast healing first, followed by base total and healing per tick; don't repeat “Instant” alongside its 1.5s GCD; place spell-wide mechanics in one description per spell card rather than repeating them in rank rows; group training source links once per spell card; and avoid repeating totals or metrics. When an expected value equals its mean, label it “Same as mean” instead of printing the same number twice. Review the rendered table at narrow and wide widths after display changes.

For each rank, the formula is:

1. Start with that rank's client BasePoints and Variance. Add PPL × (character level − BaseLevel), clamping the character level to MaxLevel, then round each base endpoint to the nearest whole heal. Lesser Heal R2 retains its tooltip-calibrated endpoint sequence and carries the fractional per-level increase through the healing-power calculation.
2. Add effective bonus healing: `(gear healing + other unlisted healing + Spiritual Guidance bonus) × rank coefficient × downrank retention`.
3. Apply relevant healing talents, round the minimum down and maximum up, then use the arithmetic midpoint of the displayed range for means.
4. Divide by effective cast time or mana cost for throughput and efficiency. Instants use a 1.5-second GCD; channels use their channel duration. Group spells use the target count, and shields are reported as absorb.

The Forever downrank rule measured from levels 18–30 is full coefficient for 17 levels after learning, then 5 percentage points less per level. Values above level 30 extrapolate that observed rule. Lesser Heal rank 1 has an additional measured 15 percentage point retention reduction. For Heal R2, the client records BasePoints 375, PPL 2.9, Variance 0.1347826123, BaseLevel 22, and MaxLevel 27. Its exact base range is 350–400 at level 22 and 364–415 at level 27 after endpoint rounding. A supplied tooltip of 439–490 is reproduced by either 104 or 105 bonus healing; the saved observation's preliminary input was 101, and the character sheet total was not shown, so that input remains unknown rather than modeled as a +3 correction.

Damage spell power combines gear and manual spell damage with one-third of bonus healing and the rank-specific Spiritual Guidance contribution from Spirit. Current-level base damage uses the client effect's BasePoints, variance, points per level, and level cap; the rank tooltip snapshot remains displayed separately. Holy Nova uses its client per-rank base range and growth, 0.107 coefficient, target count, and damage talents. Penance's instant cast channels for 2 seconds; its throughput uses that full channel time, while instant-spell talents still apply. Penance damage uses three bolt hits, each with the rank effect and 0.19 coefficient, including Rank 4's separately recorded bolt effect. Twin Disciplines affects instant casts plus Shadow Word: Death, Mind Flay, Starshards, and Penance, as specified in Blizzard's development notes. Searing Light increases Holy damage by 2%/5%, Power in Light adds 2% per rank to Penance and Smite damage against targets with Holy Fire or Shadow Word: Pain, and Power Infusion increases spell damage by 20% while active. Damage crit chance excludes healing-only Renewed Hope. Penance's Inner Focus behavior across bolts remains an explicit assumption. When Devouring Contagion and Shadowform both apply to Devouring Plague, the table marks the combined mana cost as unverified and withholds mana and damage per mana until measured in-game.

Warlock data is based on the current client spellbook and tooltip records (client build 1.60.1.70862). The planner covers 18 direct/periodic spell families and 81 ranks. Instant spells are shown as a 1.5-second GCD; cooldowns appear beside it and determine the repeat interval for sustained DPS. Death Coil's 120-second cooldown, Shadowburn's 15-second cooldown, and Conflagrate's 10-second cooldown apply to every rank. Death Coil is Affliction. [Death Coil](https://theforeverera.com/en/spells/warlock/death-coil/), [Shadowburn](https://theforeverera.com/en/spells/warlock/shadowburn/), and [Conflagrate](https://theforeverera.com/en/spells/warlock/conflagrate/) are taken from client build 1.60.1.70862. Wrack rank 1 uses the live client effect: 36 base damage at level 40 plus 2.5 per level, capped at 60, for 86 damage per tick and 516 over its six ticks at level 60 before spell power. The November 6 hotfix stops Soul Siphon from increasing Wrack's own damage; the planned November 10 Affliction tuning is not included yet. The talent and its base effect are described in [Blizzard's Warlock class notes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock). Bane of Havoc supports four explicit cases: with the marked target inside an AoE hitting `N` targets, aggregate expected damage uses `N + 0.15 × max(0, N − 1)` target-equivalents; with the mark outside the AoE it uses `1.15 × N`; a single-target hit on another enemy uses `1.15`; a hit only on the marked target uses `1`. The calculator assumes all AoE targets receive the same damage. Inferno shows 200 impact damage per cast and averages that burst across its 1-hour cooldown for sustained damage per second. It requires an Infernal Stone and summons an Infernal, but the Infernal's attacks remain outside the main DPS total because their damage and uptime depend on pet behavior. A separate per-cast pet view covers Firebolt and Lash of Pain; it uses each client base effect range plus manually entered pet spell power times the rank coefficient, then applies selected pet damage talents multiplicatively. It does not estimate pet crits, attack uptime, or total pet DPS. Its mana efficiency is omitted because its cost is 100% of base mana and base mana is not an input. Talent-granted spells remain hidden until their corresponding Settings checkbox is selected. Warlock damage talents are entered by rank under Settings: Improved Corruption and Shadow Mastery multiply their affected damage by the rank tooltip percentage; Malevolence adds its rank percentage points to Shadow crit chance; Pandemic and Ruin modify the 50% base critical damage bonus by their rank tooltip percentages; Agonizing Flames applies its listed Destruction damage multiplier and Searing Pain crit chance; Fire and Brimstone adds its listed Conflagrate crit chance. Demonic Knowledge adds 33/67/100% of character level to spell power only while its selected demon-active checkbox is on. Separate damage bonuses are multiplied together, an explicit stacking assumption. The separate pet view includes all seven Imp Firebolt ranks and all six Sayaad (Succubus/Incubus) Lash of Pain ranks. Pet spell power is a manual input. Unholy Power adds 2% per rank to pet damage; Improved Imp adds 10% to Firebolt; Improved Sayaad adds 10% per rank to Lash of Pain; Master Demonologist adds 2% per rank to the Imp or Sayaad damage it affects. Demonic Knowledge adds the selected share of character level to player and demon spell power while a demon is active. Those bonuses are multiplied as a stated stacking assumption. The per-cast view does not infer pet crit, target uptime, passive pet attacks, or total pet DPS. Healing coefficients are read from Forever client/Wowhead data where provided: common 1.5-second casts use 0.429; 3-second casts use 0.857; Lesser Heal has rank coefficients 0.429/0.571/0.714; Renew uses 1.0 total across five ticks; Prayer of Healing 0.286 per target; Holy Nova 0.107; Prayer of Mending 0.429 per successful heal; Penance 0.19 per bolt across three bolts; and Power Word: Shield 0.1. The racial direct heals use 0.429. These coefficients multiply bonus healing only; base heal amounts are added separately. Penance links use the cast ID and its healing/growth uses separate bolt-effect IDs. Lightwell's heal-over-time coefficient is not exposed in the client, so it is currently calculated with no bonus-healing scaling. Circle of Healing is a Season of Discovery rune rather than a WoW: Forever Priest spell and is excluded.

Expected healing and damage use separate crit chances and a 150% critical effect: `mean × (1 + crit chance × 0.5)`. Both start with entered sheet crit; Holy Specialization adds crit to Holy spells. Renewed Hope applies only to its listed healing spells against Weakened Soul, not to Penance damage. Ready Inner Focus adds 25 percentage points to eligible non-periodic events. Penance is modeled as three bolts over a 2-second channel; the calculation applies its instant-spell talents while dividing throughput by the full channel duration. Inner Focus's effect on each Penance bolt is an explicit assumption because the tooltip does not specify multi-bolt behavior. Penance damage-per-mana uses its full enemy-cast mana cost, not Litany of Light's healing-only refund.

Renew can crit in Forever. Its headline is the total per cast shown by the game tooltip, followed by base total and healing per tick. The displayed healing/time, expected healing, and critical-heal amount are per tick; the full 5-tick total is used for healing per mana. Penance follows the same total, base-total, per-tick order. Lightwell's critical-heal column is a single tick. Divine Aegis and Inspiration proc modeling excludes periodic ticks; Penance is treated as three direct bolt events. Divine Aegis does not stack and is capped at 1,200 per target; for Penance, any one or more critical bolts produce one per-bolt shield on the same target. The table sorts by healing and damage crit chance separately, as well as range, cast, mana, throughput, expected value, damage, Holy Binding, Divine Aegis, and Inspiration.

Talents that affect modeled outputs include Twin Disciplines, Improved Power Word: Shield, Improved Renew, Holy Specialization, Divine Fury, Spiritual Healing, Spiritual Guidance, Renewed Hope, Divine Aegis, Inspiration, Improved Healing, Mental Agility, Soul Warding, Litany of Light, Inner Focus, Searing Light's Holy-damage bonus and ready Holy Nova refund, Power in Light when its target condition is selected, Power Infusion, Spirit of Redemption's free casts, and Holy Binding. Spiritual Guidance's Spirit contribution is floored to a whole point. Holy Binding estimates the additional self-heal as half of Flash Heal when the cast targets someone else; that added component is treated as scaling with the Flash Heal's expected crit result. Renewed Hope uses the selected Weakened Soul state. Inspiration displays its armor gain and per-cast proc chance but does not estimate uptime without a cast rotation. Proc talents are modeled as ready/not-ready states, not proc probabilities. Talents that only change range, pushback, hit chance, or mana regeneration do not change the cast's healing amount. Blessed Recovery depends on incoming qualifying damage. Damage-linked healing from Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague, incoming-hit procs, and other unlisted damage-to-heal effects are outside this fixed healing-spell table. No overhealing, proc timing, healing taken, interruptions, or fight duration is modeled.
