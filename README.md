# WoW Forever Priest Throughput

A static, client-side Priest gear and healing throughput planner. Armor slots accept cloth, accessories and held items may be any armor type, and Wowhead remains the live item and loot-table reference. The item planner completes stats by item name or Wowhead ID. Gear and talent inputs are stored in browser local storage.

## Run and publish

Open `index.html` in a modern browser or serve this directory with a static file server. GitHub Pages publishes the repository root through its Actions workflow.

## Spell data and formulae

Spell rank, level, base range, cost, and Wowhead spell ID records are in `spells.js`; the user supplied tooltip observations are in `observations/annadra-level-21-22-tooltips.csv`. The records use the Forever Priest client spellbook and Wowhead Forever entries. Trainer prices are from [Warcraft Forever's Priest spell recorder](https://warcraftforever.games/classes/priest/spells).

For each rank, the formula is:

1. Start with that rank's client base healing minimum and maximum, plus per-level base growth where its metadata has been verified and the rank has not reached its cap.
2. Add effective bonus healing: `(gear healing + other unlisted healing + Spiritual Guidance bonus) × rank coefficient × downrank retention`.
3. Apply relevant healing talents, round the minimum down and maximum up, then use the arithmetic midpoint of the displayed range for means.
4. Divide by effective cast time or mana cost for throughput and efficiency. Instants use a 1.5-second GCD; channels use their channel duration. Group spells use the target count, and shields are reported as absorb.

The Forever downrank rule measured from levels 18–30 is full coefficient for 17 levels after learning, then 5 percentage points less per level. Values above level 30 extrapolate that observed rule. Lesser Heal rank 1 has an additional measured 15 percentage point retention reduction. The level 22 Heal rank 2 tooltip is 439–490, but it does not show bonus healing. An input of 104 bonus healing reproduces the tooltip without an adjustment; the prior planner input of 101 plus the former +3 fit also reproduced it. Neither is verified from the tooltip, so the planner no longer adds that fit. The client lists 2.9 base healing growth per level for Heal R2, but its level-27 endpoints are not reproduced by a simple per-endpoint 2.9 calculation. The planner shows its learned 350–400 base and marks growth above level 22 incomplete pending a better rule or paired stat observation.

Healing coefficients are read from Forever client/Wowhead data where provided: common 1.5-second casts use 0.429; 3-second casts use 0.857; Lesser Heal has rank coefficients 0.429/0.571/0.714; Renew uses 1.0 total across five ticks; Prayer of Healing 0.286 per target; Holy Nova 0.107; Prayer of Mending 0.429 per successful heal; Circle of Healing 0.214 per target; Penance 0.19 per bolt across three bolts; and Power Word: Shield 0.1. The racial direct heals use 0.429. These coefficients multiply bonus healing only; base heal amounts are added separately. Penance links use the cast ID and its healing/growth uses separate bolt-effect IDs. Lightwell's heal-over-time coefficient is not exposed in the client, so it is currently calculated with no bonus-healing scaling. Circle of Healing uses its level-60 formula range and 56% base-mana cost, converted with a 1,376 level-60 Priest base-mana assumption; its acquisition is unverified.

Critical heal expectation uses a 150% critical heal: `mean × (1 + crit chance × 0.5)`. Crit chance is the entered character-sheet spell crit plus Holy Specialization, applicable Renewed Hope, and ready Inner Focus. For Penance, the critical healing column shows one bolt's critical range; the model applies Inner Focus's +25 percentage points to all three bolts, capped at 100% per bolt. That bolt interaction is not live-verified. Divine Aegis estimates Penance absorb from the per-bolt crit distribution, applying its 1,200 target cap after aggregation; shield stacking/refresh behavior is not live-verified. Periodic Renew and Lightwell ticks are excluded. The table displays range, cast, mana, mean healing/absorb per cast time and mana, critical chance and range, expected throughput and mana efficiency, Holy Binding's extra self-heal, and expected Divine Aegis absorb. Every column header sorts ranks within its spell; card open state and sort state persist across input changes and reloads.

Talents that affect modeled outputs include Twin Disciplines, Improved Power Word: Shield, Improved Renew, Holy Specialization, Divine Fury, Spiritual Healing, Spiritual Guidance, Renewed Hope, Divine Aegis, Improved Healing, Mental Agility, Soul Warding, Litany of Light, Inner Focus, Searing Light's ready Holy Nova refund, Power Infusion, Spirit of Redemption's free casts, and Holy Binding. Spiritual Guidance's Spirit contribution is floored to a whole point. Holy Binding estimates the additional self-heal as half of Flash Heal when the cast targets someone else; that added component is treated as scaling with the Flash Heal's expected crit result. Renewed Hope uses the selected Weakened Soul state. Proc talents are modeled as ready/not-ready states, not proc probabilities. Talents that only change damage, range, pushback, armor, hit chance, or mana regeneration do not change the cast's healing amount; base spell crit can be entered directly from the character sheet. No overhealing, proc timing, healing taken, interruptions, or fight duration is modeled.

Forever-specific Priest updates can change the spellbook: [Priest changes](https://theforeverera.com/en/classes/priest/), [Forever Priest talent records](https://talentsforever.com/priest/talents), [downranking measurements and formula](https://foreverchanges.pro/downranking), and [Wowhead Forever Heal rank 2](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2055/heal). Healing ranges and inferred assumptions should be rechecked against in-game tooltips after game updates.
