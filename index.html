How the numbers work

Heal Rank 2's client data gives a midpoint of 375 at level 22, 2.9 healing per level, and 13.47826123% variance, with growth capped at level 27. That produces a 350–400 range at level 22 and 363–416 at level 27. Other rank growth uses recorded client values and caps.

Bonus healing combines gear, other entered healing, and the Spiritual Guidance bonus, then applies the rank coefficient and downrank retention. Retention stays at 100% through 17 levels above the learned level, then drops by 5 percentage points for each additional level; values above level 30 extrapolate the observed curve. Lesser Heal Rank 1 keeps its tooltip-fitted exception. Talent healing multipliers apply after base and bonus healing; the low and high endpoints round down and up.

Throughput and mana efficiency use the midpoint of the displayed healing range. Heal and Greater Heal cast times include Divine Fury; instant spells use a 1.5-second global cooldown.

Expected healing and damage = mean × [1 + (critical chance × 0.5)], assuming critical effects deal 150%. Critical chance includes entered sheet chance, Holy Specialization for Holy spells, Renewed Hope on eligible heals when Weakened Soul is present, and ready Inner Focus for eligible direct events. Penance is evaluated as three bolt events; its bolt-by-bolt Inner Focus behavior remains unverified.

Renew can critically heal in Forever, and Renew and Lightwell show a critical tick or event amount. Divine Aegis and Inspiration can trigger from direct critical heals; Penance's three bolts hit one target, so its estimate counts one Aegis when one or more bolts crit. Inspiration shows its armor bonus and per-cast proc chance, but not uptime without a cast rotation. Divine Aegis does not stack and is capped at 1,200 per target.

Prayer of Mending counts successful heals: one initial heal and up to five transfers. Talent ranks require their listed points in Holy or Discipline; later ranks require the talent and trainer. Lesser Heal Ranks 2–3 are marked as a source conflict: UrGameTips lists trainer prices, while the Warcraft Forever beta spell index says Lesser Heal ranks are not taught by a trainer. Lightwell's acquisition is also unresolved: the Forever spell index marks it not taught by a trainer, while MetaBot lists trainer levels. Their prices stay unknown. Unknown trainer prices remain unknown, not free. This fixed-spell table excludes damage-linked healing such as Vampiric Embrace and incoming-hit procs such as Blessed Recovery.
