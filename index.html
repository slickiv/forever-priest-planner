How the numbers work

The displayed healing range is the client base range plus bonus healing × the spell coefficient at each endpoint. Mean healing is the midpoint of that range, and mean healing per time and per mana use that value. Power Word: Shield uses 10% of bonus healing and reports absorb per cast time and absorb per mana. An instant uses the 1.5 sec global cooldown, including Renew; its total healing is displayed per cast and its tick duration is called out. Healing per mana uses total healing divided by displayed mana cost. Group spells use the target count above. Values are theoretical, before talents, critical heals, overhealing, and target limits. Gear totals with “+ ?” include known stats only; fill the unknown slots for a complete comparison. The costs shown are base costs; the reduction control applies Improved Healing to eligible spells.

CHARACTER IMPORT

Import from Armory

Imports the public profile’s equipped gear, then checks Wowhead for healing effects the Armory omits (such as Staff of Orgrimmar). No Blizzard credentials are sent. Wowhead item and Armory profile requests pass through a read-only CORS relay. If the relay is unavailable, paste the page source: open the Armory, press Ctrl+U (⌘+Option+U on Mac), select all, copy, then paste here.
