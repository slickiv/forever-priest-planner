How the numbers work

Heal Rank 2's client data gives a midpoint of 375 at level 22, 2.9 healing per level, and 13.47826123% variance, with growth capped at level 27. That produces a 350–400 range at level 22 and 363–416 at level 27. Other rank growth uses recorded client values and caps.

Bonus healing combines gear, other entered healing, and the Spiritual Guidance bonus, then applies the rank coefficient and downrank retention. Retention stays at 100% through 17 levels above the learned level, then drops by 5 percentage points for each additional level; values above level 30 extrapolate the observed curve. Lesser Heal Rank 1 keeps its tooltip-fitted exception. Talent healing multipliers apply after base and bonus healing; the low and high endpoints round down and up.

Throughput and mana efficiency use the midpoint of the displayed healing range. Heal and Greater Heal cast times include Divine Fury; instant spells use a 1.5-second global cooldown.

Expected healing = mean × [1 + (critical chance × 0.5)], assuming critical heals deal 150%. Critical chance includes entered sheet chance, Holy Specialization for Holy spells, Renewed Hope on eligible heals when Weakened Soul is present, and ready Inner Focus on eligible non-periodic spells.

Renew can critically heal in Forever, and Renew and Lightwell show a critical tick or event amount. Divine Aegis and Inspiration only trigger from non-periodic critical heals. Inspiration shows its armor bonus and per-target trigger chance, but not uptime without a cast rotation. Divine Aegis does not stack and is capped at 1,200 per target. Penance's three bolts hit one target, so its estimate counts one shield when one or more bolts crit. Inner Focus is modeled as adding 25 percentage points to a Penance cast; its live bolt-by-bolt behavior is unverified.

Prayer of Mending counts successful heals: one initial heal and up to five transfers. Circle of Healing uses a level-60 formula; its acquisition and Lightwell's acquisition are unverified. An unquoted trainer price is unknown, not free. This fixed-spell table excludes damage-linked healing such as Vampiric Embrace and incoming-hit procs such as Blessed Recovery.

These calculations draw on downranking measurements, Heal Rank 2 client data, its generated client effect, Forever Priest talent descriptions, Forever Priest spell changes, and Blizzard talent notes. Results are theoretical and do not account for overhealing, proc timing, interruptions, or fight duration.

Import from Armory

Looks up the public WoW Forever US profile by character name, imports equipped gear, then checks Wowhead for healing effects the Armory omits. No Blizzard credentials are sent. Requests pass through a read-only CORS relay. If the relay is unavailable, paste the page source: open the Armory, press Ctrl+U (⌘+Option+U on Mac), select all, copy, then paste here.
