Base values use Forever spell data. Bonus healing and shield spell power use their spell coefficients. Instants use a 1.5 sec global cooldown for throughput. Group healing is shown per target, with party totals in the details.

Healing Absorb
How the numbers work

Healing per cast time = (base healing + bonus healing × coefficient) ÷ cast time. Power Word: Shield uses 10% of bonus healing and reports absorb per cast time and absorb per mana. An instant uses the 1.5 sec global cooldown, including Renew; its total healing is displayed per cast and its tick duration is called out. Healing per mana uses total healing divided by displayed mana cost. Group spells use the target count above. Values are theoretical, before talents, critical heals, overhealing, and target limits. The costs shown are base costs; the reduction control applies Improved Healing to eligible spells.
