Healing Damage Absorb
How the numbers work

Base heal ranges use each rank's client BasePoints, points per level, variance, and BaseLevel/MaxLevel. Level growth stops at MaxLevel. The client applies points per level to both range endpoints, then the displayed base endpoints round to the nearest whole heal. Healing power and talents apply after that base range.

Bonus healing combines gear, other entered healing, and the Spiritual Guidance bonus, then applies the rank coefficient and downrank retention. Retention stays at 100% through 17 levels above the learned level, then drops by 5 percentage points for each additional level; values above level 30 extrapolate the observed curve. Lesser Heal Rank 1 keeps its tooltip-fitted exception. Talent healing multipliers apply after base and bonus healing; the low and high endpoints round down and up.

Throughput and mana efficiency use the midpoint of the displayed healing range. Heal and Greater Heal cast times include Divine Fury; instant spells use a 1.5-second global cooldown.

Expected healing and damage = mean × [1 + (critical chance × 0.5)], assuming critical effects deal 150%. Healing and damage use separate crit chances. Both include entered sheet chance, Holy Specialization adds chance to Holy spells, and ready Inner Focus adds 25 percentage points to eligible direct events. Renewed Hope applies to its listed healing spells when Weakened Soul is present; it does not increase damage crit chance.

Renew can critically heal in Forever, and Renew and Lightwell show a critical tick or event amount. Divine Aegis and Inspiration can trigger from direct critical heals; Penance's three bolts hit one target, so its estimate counts one Aegis when one or more bolts crit. Inspiration shows its armor bonus and per-cast proc chance, but not uptime without a cast rotation. Divine Aegis does not stack and is capped at 1,200 per target.

Prayer of Mending counts successful heals: one initial heal and up to five transfers. Talent-granted spells appear only when selected in Settings; later ranks require the talent and trainer. Trainer prices appear only where a source provides them. This fixed-spell table excludes damage-linked healing such as Vampiric Embrace and incoming-hit procs such as Blessed Recovery.

Every displayed rank's level was compared by spell name and rank with the current Priest level list. Spell IDs, talent requirements, and trainer information were cross-referenced with current per-rank spell records. reference · reference · reference · link

Holy Nova damage growth uses its rank-specific client base range and per-level effects. Penance's 3-bolt healing total and damage total are separate from per-bolt and per-tick amounts. It is an instant spell with a 2-second channel: instant-spell talents apply, while throughput uses the full channel duration. Damage applies Twin Disciplines, Searing Light, conditional Power in Light, and active Power Infusion. Penance damage-per-mana uses the full enemy-cast cost, not Litany of Light's healing-only refund. Holy Nova damage is shown per target; damage per second multiplies by the entered enemy count.

Damage
Damage calculations

Rank tooltip snapshots are shown separately from modeled base damage at your selected level. The model uses each client effect's base points, variance, points per level, and level cap; ranks without a client growth effect use the listed tooltip snapshot. Spell power combines equipped and manually entered spell damage, one-third of healing power, and Spiritual Guidance's rank-specific damage share of total Spirit (1/3/5/6/8%). Dark Inspiration can temporarily increase Spirit by 3% per rank; select its buff when active. The damage column adds spell power times the client coefficient, with downrank retention, then applies applicable talent multipliers. DoT spell power applies per tick across the effect duration; Improved Shadow Word: Pain adds one tick per rank (3 seconds). Improved Mind Flay compounds each later tick by 10% or 20% over the preceding tick.

Expected damage applies the entered crit chance and Holy Specialization to each direct hit and each client-flagged periodic tick. Ready Inner Focus adds 25 percentage points to eligible direct hits and makes that cast free; a ready Searing Light proc makes Holy Nova free. Ordinary critical effects use 150%; Shadowform makes Shadow spell critical hits deal 200% of normal damage. Expected damage per second divides expected damage by the longest of cast/GCD, cooldown, and DoT duration, assuming full DoT uptime. Twin Disciplines follows its instant-spell rule plus Blizzard's named exceptions: Shadow Word: Death, Mind Flay, Starshards, and Penance. Mind Blast includes its reduced cooldown when Improved Mind Blast is selected; Mana Burn includes Improved Mana Burn cast reductions; Improved Healing and Mental Agility reduce the eligible damage spells' mana costs; Devouring Contagion reduces Devouring Plague mana cost by 25% or 50%. The model assumes hits land and does not add target resistance.

Holy Nova is per enemy target, Penance is per bolt and per three-bolt cast, Holy Fire separates its direct hit and periodic component, and Mana Burn damage equals half the Mana actually drained. Shadow Word: Death lists its possible self-backlash outside outgoing damage. Devouring Contagion's reduced mana cost is included; target-death spread damage is excluded from the single-target comparison. Chastise is Dwarf-only and Humanoid-only. Feedback is Human-only, Shadowguard Troll-only, Starshards Night Elf-only, and Touch of Weakness Undead-only.

Rank levels, mana, cast times, cooldowns, and restrictions are cross-referenced against Wowhead Forever spell records. Bonus healing adds one third as much spell damage under Blizzard's itemization rules. Damage spell training prices are not yet verified for this list. reference · reference · reference · reference · reference
