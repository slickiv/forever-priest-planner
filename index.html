How the numbers work

Heal R2's client effect has midpoint 375 at level 22, 2.9 healing growth per level, 13.47826123% variance, and a level-27 cap. The range is rounded from midpoint × (1 ± variance/2); this yields 350–400 at level 22 and 363–416 at level 27. Other listed per-level growth uses client values and recorded caps. Bonus healing = (gear + other healing + Spiritual Guidance bonus) × rank coefficient × downrank retention. Retention stays at 100% through 17 levels above the learned level, then falls by 5 percentage points per level; above level 30 this observed curve is extrapolated. Lesser Heal R1 keeps its tooltip-fitted exception. Talent healing multipliers are applied to the base plus bonus-healing result; final low/high values round down/up. Throughput and mana efficiency use the midpoint of that displayed range. Cast time includes Divine Fury for Heal/Greater Heal; instants use a 1.5 sec GCD. Expected healing = mean × (1 + crit chance × 0.5), assuming a 150% critical heal. Crit chance includes the entered sheet value, Holy Specialization for Holy-school spells, Renewed Hope on eligible heals when Weakened Soul is present, and ready Inner Focus on non-periodic eligible spells. Renew can crit in Forever; Renew and Lightwell display a critical tick/event amount. Divine Aegis and Inspiration only trigger from non-periodic critical heals. Inspiration displays its armor bonus and per-target trigger chance; it does not estimate buff uptime without a cast rotation. Divine Aegis is non-stacking and capped at 1,200 per target; Penance's three bolts share one target, so the estimate counts one shield if one or more bolts crit. Inner Focus is modeled as applying +25 percentage points to a Penance cast; live bolt-by-bolt behavior remains unverified. Prayer of Mending counts successful heals: one initial heal and up to five transfers. Circle of Healing uses a level-60 formula; its acquisition and Lightwell's acquisition are unverified. An unquoted trainer price is unknown, not free. The fixed-spell table excludes damage-linked healing such as Vampiric Embrace and incoming-hit procs such as Blessed Recovery. Read the downranking measurements, Heal R2 client data, Heal R2 generated client effect, Forever Priest talent descriptions, Forever Priest spell changes, and Blizzard talent notes. This is theoretical output before overhealing, proc timing, interruptions, or fight duration.

CHARACTER IMPORT

Import from Armory

Looks up the public WoW Forever US profile by character name, imports equipped gear, then checks Wowhead for healing effects the Armory omits. No Blizzard credentials are sent. Requests pass through a read-only CORS relay. If the relay is unavailable, paste the page source: open the Armory, press Ctrl+U (⌘+Option+U on Mac), select all, copy, then paste here.
